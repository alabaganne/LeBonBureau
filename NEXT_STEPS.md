# LeBonBureau — Next Steps: Marketing, Samples & Pilot Batch

> Goal: move from a working e-commerce MVP to validated real-world demand — by producing physical desk samples, building a small test batch, and running first marketing experiments.

---

## 0. Where we are today

- ✅ E-commerce platform live (Next.js shop + Medusa backend): landing, product, cart, checkout, Medusa admin.
- ✅ Product catalogue, stock and orders managed in Medusa.
- ⬜ No physical product validated yet.
- ⬜ No paying customers / no marketing channels active.

**The big open question:** *Will people pay for these desks?* Everything below is designed to answer that as cheaply and quickly as possible.

---

## 1. Decide the goal of this phase

Pick ONE primary objective before spending money. Recommended:

> **"Get 10 desks into the hands of real users and collect proof (photos, quotes, pre-orders) that validates demand."**

Secondary metrics to watch:
- Cost to acquire a lead / customer (CAC)
- Conversion rate (visitor → email → order)
- Production cost per desk vs. target retail price
- Qualitative feedback (build quality, assembly, fit in a home/office)

---

## 2. Samples first (1–3 units)

Before committing to 10, build **1–3 hero samples** to de-risk production and create marketing assets.

- [ ] Finalize the 1–2 SKUs to prototype (the strongest sellers from the catalogue).
- [ ] Source a maker / workshop / contract manufacturer; get a quote per unit at qty 1, 10, 50.
- [ ] Lock materials, finish, dimensions, and a target landed cost.
- [ ] Build the sample(s). Document the process (you'll reuse this content).
- [ ] Stress-test: assembly time, stability, surface durability, packaging/shipping survivability.
- [ ] **Photograph properly** — this is your single most valuable marketing asset. See §5.

**Exit criteria:** sample looks sellable, you know your unit cost, and you have great photos.

---

## 3. Pilot batch of 10 desks

Once a sample passes, produce a small batch to test real fulfillment.

- [ ] Confirm per-unit cost at qty 10 and total capital required.
- [ ] Decide allocation of the 10 units:
  - 2–3 → keep as demo/showroom + photo/video units
  - 1–2 → gift to micro-influencers / design accounts in exchange for content
  - 5–7 → sell (or pre-sell) to real customers at full or "founder" price
- [ ] Set up packaging + a shipping method; get real shipping costs (desks are heavy/bulky).
- [ ] Define return/warranty policy for pilot buyers.
- [ ] Track each unit: who got it, channel, feedback, did they pay.

**Exit criteria:** at least a few units sold to strangers (not friends/family) and a clear read on unit economics including shipping.

---

## 4. Validate demand BEFORE/ALONGSIDE building 10

Don't wait for inventory to test the market. Run these in parallel with §2–3:

- [ ] **Pre-order / waitlist page** — add a "Reserve yours" or "Join the waitlist" CTA to the live site. A small deposit (e.g. refundable) is the strongest demand signal.
- [ ] **Landing page experiment** — drive a tiny ad budget (€50–150) to the product page and measure click → email → checkout intent.
- [ ] **Email capture** everywhere — collect interested visitors even if they don't buy yet.

> A waitlist of 50 emails or 5 paid deposits tells you more than 10 finished desks sitting in a garage.

---

## 5. Content & marketing assets to create

Built once, reused across every channel:

- [ ] **Hero product photos** — clean studio shots + lifestyle/in-context (desk in a real room).
- [ ] **Short video** — 15–30s build/reveal or "day in the office" clip for Reels/TikTok/Shorts.
- [ ] **Founder story / why** — short written + video. People buy from people.
- [ ] **Spec sheet** — dimensions, materials, weight capacity, assembly time, warranty.
- [ ] **3–5 customer-facing benefit statements** (not features — outcomes).

---

## 6. First marketing channels (start with 1–2, not all)

Pick the channels where your buyer actually is. Recommended starting pair: **Instagram/TikTok organic + a small paid test.**

| Channel | Why | First action |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / TikTok (organic) | Visual product, cheap, build audience | Post sample photos/video; 3×/week |
| Paid social (Meta/TikTok ads) | Fast demand signal | €50–150 test to waitlist/product page |
| Local / community | Desks ship poorly far away early on | Local marketplaces, coworking spaces, design fairs |
| Micro-influencers | Borrowed trust, content | Gift 1–2 pilot units for posts |
| Email | Owned channel, converts | Capture leads now, simple welcome sequence |

**Avoid early:** broad SEO, paid Google Shopping at scale, big agency spend. Too slow / too expensive for validation.

---

## 7. Track-2: operational readiness (lightweight)

So you can actually fulfill orders when they come:

- [ ] Payment flow tested end-to-end with a real card (small live transaction).
- [ ] Order confirmation + shipping-update emails working.
- [ ] Admin can see/manage incoming orders.
- [ ] Shipping cost & lead-time stated clearly on the product page.
- [ ] Basic terms: returns, warranty, delivery window.

---

## 8. 30 / 60 / 90 day rough timeline

**Days 1–30 — Sample + signal**
- Build 1–2 samples, shoot photos/video.
- Launch waitlist + email capture on the live site.
- Start organic social; run one small paid test.

**Days 31–60 — Pilot batch + first sales**
- Produce 10 units once sample passes.
- Pre-sell / sell from the waitlist; gift 1–2 to influencers.
- Verify full checkout + fulfillment with real orders.

**Days 61–90 — Decide**
- Review: unit economics, CAC, conversion, qualitative feedback.
- **Go / iterate / pivot** decision on whether to scale production.

---

## 9. Decision gates (don't skip)

1. **After samples:** Is the product sellable and is unit cost viable? → if no, fix product/cost first.
2. **After demand test:** Are people signaling real intent (deposits/waitlist/sales)? → if no, fix offer/audience before building 10.
3. **After pilot:** Do the economics work including shipping & returns? → if yes, scale; if marginal, iterate; if no, pivot.

---

## 10. Open questions to answer

- [ ] Who exactly is the buyer? (remote workers? designers? small offices?)
- [ ] What's the target retail price, and does the margin survive shipping?
- [ ] Local-first or ship nationally from day one?
- [ ] What's the realistic budget for this phase (samples + 10 units + ad test)?
- [ ] Who handles fulfillment/packing/shipping?
